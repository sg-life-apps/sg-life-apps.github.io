SG Bus Support

中文

SG Bus shows real-time Singapore bus arrivals, your favourite stops, nearby stops, full bus routes on a map and MRT service status. It is available on iPhone, iPad, Android and the web at sg-bus.vercel.app.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or feature requests: cgpanda.sg@gmail.com. Please include your device model, app version (shown on the About screen) and the bus stop code if relevant.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the arrival data come from?

From the Land Transport Authority's LTA DataMall. Arrival times are estimates and refresh automatically every 30 seconds.

Why does a bus show "Arr" or a dash?

"Arr" means the bus is arriving now. A dash means LTA has no estimate for that bus yet, for example outside operating hours.

What do the colours mean?

Green: seats available. Yellow: standing available. Red: limited standing.

How do I save a favourite stop?

Open a bus stop and tap the heart. Favourites appear on the home screen with live arrivals.

Why does the app ask for my location?

Only to show bus stops near you. Your location stays on your device and is never sent to our servers. You can use every other feature without granting location access.

Nearby stops or arrivals are not loading

Check your internet connection and refresh the screen. If location is denied, enable it in Settings → SG Bus → Location.

常见问题

到站数据来自哪里？

来自新加坡陆路交通管理局（LTA）的 LTA DataMall。到站时间为预估值，每 30 秒自动刷新。

为什么显示「Arr」或横线？

「Arr」表示车辆即将到站。横线表示 LTA 暂无该班车的预估时间，例如在非运营时段。

颜色代表什么？

绿色：有座位；黄色：可站立；红色：拥挤。

如何收藏站点？

打开一个巴士站，点爱心即可。收藏的站点会在首页显示实时到站。

为什么需要位置权限？

仅用于显示你附近的巴士站。位置信息只在你的设备上使用，不会发送到我们的服务器。不授权位置也可以使用其他所有功能。

附近站点或到站信息加载不出来

请检查网络连接后刷新页面。如果拒绝了位置权限，可在「设置 → SG Bus → 位置」中开启。
